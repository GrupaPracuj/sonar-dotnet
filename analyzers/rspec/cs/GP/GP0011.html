A 401 (Unauthorized) response means the caller's identity could not be established or its credentials were rejected. A 403 (Forbidden) response means the caller is known, but is not allowed to perform the requested operation.

Returning 401 from a permission/role check (e.g. IsInRole, HasClaim), or returning 403 from an authentication check (e.g. Identity.IsAuthenticated), mixes up these two distinct failure reasons and can mislead API clients about how to react to the error.

Return 403 (Forbid) when the check is about what the caller is allowed to do, and 401 (Unauthorized) when the check is about whether the caller is authenticated at all.
